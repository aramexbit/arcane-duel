ARCANE DUEL: Warriors of the Lost Realm

An original game — characters, art, music and code created for the make.fun best-game-01 bounty.

Art: original procedural sprites drawn in code. Audio: Web Audio synthesis.

How to Play

Goal: Blast your rival to 0 HP before they blast you. Turns alternate.
Aim: Move the mouse — the dotted line previews your shot. ↑↓ angle, ←→ power.
Fire: Space or click. Weapons: Q/E or click the inventory.
Skills: 1 2 use skills (cost energy), 3 ultimate — only at FULL energy.
Wind: shown at the top — it pushes every projectile. Wind changes every turn.
Terrain: explosions carve craters. Heroes fall if their ground is destroyed.
Combo: fire hitting a purple arcane zone triggers an ELEMENTAL COMBO blast!
Pause: Esc   Mute: M
