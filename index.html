How to Play

Goal: Blast your rival to 0 HP before they blast you. Turns alternate — each shooting turn lasts 15 seconds.
Delay queue: every action costs time — the player with the lowest clock acts next. Light weapons are quick (Sting Dart +90), heavy ones are slow (Horn Cannon +160), skills +130, ultimate +200, Full Power shots +60 extra. A heavy action can let your rival act twice before you do — the turn banner says so ("⚡ TURN AGAIN!"). Passing (+60) and Shield (+60) are cheap, so you act sooner. The cost of your next shot is shown under your player card.
Aim sensitivity: tune it in Settings (0.5× steady — 2.0× twitchy). It scales the AIM pad drag and field/mouse drag aim. At 0.5× a field drag builds power slowly — use the AIM pad or hold Space to charge to 100.
Aim: Move the mouse — the dotted line previews your shot (fan shows the aim arc). ↑↓ angle, ←→ power (0–100). V toggles the preview.
Move: Hold A/D to walk (150 steps per turn). Reposition before you shoot!
Fire: Click, tap the 🔥 FIRE button, or hold Space to charge power then release to fire. Fire at POWER 100 for a golden FULL POWER move — a different special for every weapon! When POW burns, click the 🔥 POW FIRE! button.
Weapons: Q/E or click the inventory. Every weapon has its own weight and wind resistance — heavy shots fall fast but ignore the wind, light shots drift. Buy more in the 🛒 Weapon Shop with coins earned from wins.
Skills: 1 2 use skills (cost energy), 3 ultimate — only at FULL energy.
Utilities: T Teleport — click a spot within range (Nyx: cheaper). R Reflect Trap — enemy shots bounce back at them. H Heal +25 HP. Teleport and Heal END your turn — no shooting after using one!
Pass / Emote: P Pass — skip your turn without shooting. G Emote — show a fun bubble above your hero (free, turn continues).
Wind: shown at the top — it pushes every projectile. Wind changes every turn.
Terrain: explosions carve craters. Heroes fall if their ground is destroyed — blast the ground clean through and your rival falls into the abyss and dies!
Combo: fire hitting a purple arcane zone triggers an ELEMENTAL COMBO blast!
Campaign: beat 6 battles — beetles, wasps and shadow scorpions from the Mist Forest — then face the FINAL BOSS, Elite Bront with 150 HP! Your progress auto-saves — Continue Campaign on the menu resumes where you left off.
Survival: endless waves, one foe per wave — each wave +10 HP and +5% damage for the enemy, difficulty climbing Apprentice → Elite. Every wave cleared pays +25 🪙. Your best wave is saved.
Challenges: three fixed duels with a special win condition — One-Shot (win with a single shot), No Miss (win without a single MISS), Speed Run (win within 6 of your own turns). Complete one for +50 🪙.
Weather: snow drifts in Crystal Valley, embers rise in Ember Canyon, light motes float in the Sky Temple. Graphics quality (Settings) controls particles and weather — Low turns weather off.
Offline: the game installs as an app (PWA) and works fully offline after the first load. Export / Import your save from Settings. Touch (phone): left virtual joystick to walk, drag on the field to aim, big 🔥 FIRE button. 🎯 AIM pad (left, above the joystick): hold it to aim — drag up for a higher arc, drag right for more power — release to fire! Power is shown as a small bar above your hero. Tap II to pause, 🔍 ZOOM OUT to see the whole arena (or press C). Two fingers work at once — steer with one thumb, aim and fire with the other.
POINT / POW: hitting the enemy fills your POINT meter (+8 per hit, +25 every turn). At 100 POINT a 💥 POW! button appears — tap it to unleash your ultimate jurus!
Skins: buy weapon skins in the 🛒 Weapon Shop with 💎 Gems earned from battles — damage ÷ 2, +2 per hit, +50 win bonus, +25 per win in your win streak (loss: +10). The game-over screen shows the full breakdown. Skins are cosmetic only — they change the look, trail, explosion and sound, never the damage. Selecting a skin auto-plays its Full Power preview! ⭐ Featured: one skin each week at 20% off. 🎨 Skin Collection: browse everything you own, filter by rarity, equip from the collection. Elements: strong wind (6+) feeds fire blasts (+25% radius, "FIRE+WIND!"). The Elemental Beam chills — a hit SLOWs the foe's next turn (half moves). Homing Orbs chain lightning to another hero within 120px. Earth blasts dig deeper craters and raise rims at the edges.
